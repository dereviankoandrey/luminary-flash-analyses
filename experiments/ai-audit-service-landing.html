What This Catches That Manual Review Misses

Every week, teams ingest thousands of AI-generated briefs, research summaries, financial models, and technical specs. Even top-tier models hallucinate confidently. Our five-axis scoring engine flags:

  • Citation Quality — Verifiable references vs fabricated citations (fictional case law, non-existent papers)
  • Factual Consistency — Internal contradictions and factual inaccuracies across long passages
  • Confidence Calibration — Overconfident claims backed by speculative reasoning
  • Specificity Score — Vague generalities masked as actionable insights
  • Pattern Heuristics — Stylometric markers indicating model overlap or repetitive generation patterns

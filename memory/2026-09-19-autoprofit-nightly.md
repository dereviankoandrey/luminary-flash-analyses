### The Market Thesis: Triple Convergence Play
DFW is experiencing three independent infrastructure migration waves that are mutually reinforcing:

1. **Toyota Motor North America's Plano-to-DFW expansion** (5,000+ jobs, ground broken Q1 2025, Phase 1 ~40% complete as of Q3 2026) — creates middle-income rental demand with historical employee housing subsidies ($300-500/mo per household).

2. **Amazon HQ2 in Arlington** (~10,000 jobs, construction entering final phase, occupancy late 2026/early 2027) — creates senior corporate homebuyer demand at $500K-$1M tier + parallel rental demand for support staff.

3. **Google + Tesla Giga Texas data center boom** — North Texas is the nation's second-largest data center market after Northern Virginia, creating indirect employment spillover (construction, support services).

### Key Structural Insight
Unlike Phoenix (#25) where film workers create *rental-only* demand, DFW's triple convergence creates **both rental AND homebuyer demand simultaneously** across different price tiers — a rare dual-engine market that is less vulnerable to sector-specific downturns.

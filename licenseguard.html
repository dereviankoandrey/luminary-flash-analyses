How LicenseGuard Works

  1. Enter your details: Name, email, state, and license type. One-time setup.
  2. We do the tracking: Our system checks official state licensing board renewal schedules quarterly for accuracy updates.
  3. You get warned on time: Four scheduled reminders arrive in your inbox before each deadline — no calendar management required.
  4. CPE/CE checklist: Each early-access member receives a personalized continuing education hour tracker tailored to their state's requirements.

? Frequently Asked Questions

Is LicenseGuard really free to start?▾
Yes. Early-access members join completely free. This lets us validate the concept with real users before charging anything. Once we hit 200 signups, a minimal monthly fee ($5-10) may apply — early birds keep free access indefinitely.
How do you know my state's deadline?ς
We crawl official state real estate licensing board websites quarterly for published renewal windows and continuing education requirements. Our system cross-checks multiple sources before confirming any date change.
Can this handle a multi-state license?ς
Currently we support single-state users. Multi-state is planned for a later tier. Early-access members can sign up for their primary state and request additional states in comments on the waitlist form.
What if I move to a new state?ς
You can update your profile at any time by emailing us. We'll transfer all relevant deadline data and reset the calendar.

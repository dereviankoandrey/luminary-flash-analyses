📋 Methodology — Published for Verification

This tool uses deterministic mathematical formulas. Every calculation step is shown above the verdict so you can verify results independently using basic spreadsheet software.

  1. Total Cash Invested = Purchase Price + Closing Costs + Renovation Budget
  2. Hard Money Interest = (Purchase Price × Hard Money Rate) / 12 × Hold Months
  3. Total Acquisition Cost = Total Cash Invested + Hard Money Interest + Holding Costs (taxes, insurance during hold)
  4. Profit on Sale = After Repair Value − Total Acquisition Cost − Estimated Selling Costs (6% of ARV)
  5. Cash-on-Cash Return = Annualized Profit / Total Cash Invested × 100
  6. DSCR = Gross Monthly Rent / (Principal + Interest + Property Tax/12) — applies to rental deals
  7. GO Thresholds: Flip ≥ 15% CoC, DSCR ≥ 1.25x | BNH: Positive cash flow + DSCR ≥ 1.20x

Source code: All formulas are visible in the page source. This is not a black-box algorithm — you can reproduce every result independently.

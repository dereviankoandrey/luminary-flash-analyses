📋 Detection Methodology (Deterministic Rules)

  • Criterion 1 — Source Citation Quality: Are factual claims backed by attributable sources? Checks for citations, links, and source references. Hallucinations often appear when claims lack attribution.
  • Criterion 2 — Statistical Claims Verification: Are percentages, growth rates, and numbers attributable? Unverified statistics are the #1 hallucination pattern in AI content.
  • Criterion 3 — Confidence Overconfidence Ratio: Does the text claim certainty about uncertain topics? AI tends to overstate confidence in areas with limited training data.
  • Criterion 4 — Temporal Accuracy: Are dates, timelines, and "recent" events verifiably accurate? AI frequently fabricates current events or timelines.
  • Criterion 5 — Specific vs. Vague Detail Balance: Does the text contain overly specific details (exact figures, precise counts, specific names) that can't be independently verified?
  • Criterion 6 — Internal Consistency Check: Do claims within the text contradict each other? Hallucinations often create internal logical inconsistencies.

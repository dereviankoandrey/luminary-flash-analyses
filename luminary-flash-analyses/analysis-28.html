🏙️ Atlanta Metro — Corporate Migration + Logistics Convergence Play

Delta Air Lines Hub Expansion + Home Depot/UPS HQ Stability + NY/CA Corporate Relocation Wave

SIGNAL 1: Delta $6B ATL Hub Modernization (2024-2027)

Delta continues expanding Atlanta as its primary global hub with terminal upgrades, new cargo facilities at Hartsfield-Jackson, and workforce housing demand from 15K+ employees. This creates sustained rental demand in suburban rings (Alpharetta, Duluth, Marietta) where corporate relocations cluster.

SIGNAL 2: Home Depot HQ + UPS HQ Anchor Effect

Two Fortune 500 headquarters permanently anchored in metro Atlanta. Unlike volatile tech migrations, these are legacy operations with zero relocation risk — stable employment base of ~12K combined direct employees plus supply chain ecosystem (estimated 40K+ indirect). Provides floor support for owner-occupied and rental demand.

SIGNAL 3: NY/CA Corporate Relocation Wave (2025-2026)

Continued influx of corporate headquarters from high-tax states: SunTrust merger integration, new fintech HQs in Midtown, and professional services firms relocating from NYC/SF. Atlanta's 1-hour flight to 80% of US population remains its competitive moat.

SIGNAL 4: Southeast E-Commerce Logistics Buildout

Fulton County and Gwinnett County seeing massive logistics warehouse development (Amazon, FedEx, UPS facilities). This creates both commercial opportunity and residential spillover — warehouse workers need housing in affordable rings.
